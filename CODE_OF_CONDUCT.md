# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in communication.
- Assume positive intent and focus on technical outcomes.
- Provide actionable feedback and avoid personal attacks.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and different experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Personal insults, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers using the contact path in `SECURITY.md`.

All reports will be reviewed and handled in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
